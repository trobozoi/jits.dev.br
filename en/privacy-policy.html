Privacy Policy

Updated on November 14, 2026

This policy explains how JITS (Jailton Information Technology Solutions), from Fortaleza, Brazil, handles the personal data of people who visit jits.dev.br and get in touch through the contact form, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, the LGPD).

What data we collect

We only collect the data you provide yourself in the contact form:

  • name;
  • email;
  • company and WhatsApp number, if you choose to provide them;
  • the type of project and the message you write.

The site does not use cookies, has no advertising tools and does not build visitor profiles.

What we use it for

We use this data only to reply to your message, understand what you need and, if you ask, prepare a proposal or quote. The legal basis is your consent, given when you tick the box in the form, and the preliminary steps for a possible contract at the request of the data subject (LGPD, article 7, items I and V).

We do not sell, rent or share your data for marketing purposes.

Who else has access

For the site to work, we use some third-party services that process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms: receives the form submission and forwards the message to our email;
  • Zoho Mail: hosts the contato@jits.dev.br mailbox, where the message is stored;
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the site and may log technical access data, such as IP address, for security;
  • Google Fonts: provides the site's fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers;
  • WhatsApp: if you use the WhatsApp button, the conversation happens in the app and follows its rules.

Some of these services may store data outside Brazil, always within the cases allowed by the LGPD.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as needed to handle your request and follow a possible business relationship. After that, the data is deleted, unless the law requires keeping it longer.

Your rights

Under the LGPD, you can at any time:

  • confirm whether we process your data and request access to it;
  • correct incomplete or outdated data;
  • request deletion of your data;
  • withdraw the consent you gave;
  • ask for information about who the data was shared with.

To make any of these requests, write to contato@jits.dev.br. You can also contact the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Security

The site runs over a secure connection (HTTPS), and access to received messages is restricted to JITS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the update date.
