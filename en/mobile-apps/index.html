When it makes sense

Signs that an app will help

  • Your field team writes on paper and types it all again at the office.
  • Customers ask to follow orders or services on their phone.
  • Sales arrive by chat message and get lost in conversations.
  • You want to notify customers the moment something changes.
  • Your competitors already serve customers on mobile.
  • The website cannot handle what customers need to do day to day.

Common questions

Questions we always hear

Does the app run on Android and iPhone?

Yes. We use React Native, so the same project produces the app for both platforms.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. We handle publishing to Google Play and the App Store. The developer accounts stay in your company's name.

Can the app use data from my current system?

Yes. We integrate with the system you already use and, if needed, build the API that connects the two.

What happens after the app is published?

We continue with maintenance, updates and new features as needed.
